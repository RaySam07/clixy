- [OPEN:spotify:search:WORDS] opens spotify straight on a search for those words (spaces are fine); [OPEN:spotify] just opens it.

## keys (with spotify in front)
- space play or pause · ctrl+right next · ctrl+left previous · ctrl+up and ctrl+down volume · ctrl+s shuffle · ctrl+r repeat

## where things are
- liked songs and playlists: your library on the left · the queue, lyrics and connect to a device (play on a speaker or phone): the icons at the bottom right
- settings (crossfade, autoplay, start spotify when windows starts): your picture at the top right > settings · private session: same menu
- downloading for offline (premium): the download arrow on a playlist or album
