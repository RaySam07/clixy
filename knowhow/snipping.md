- win+shift+s (or [OPEN:ms-screenclip:]) dims the screen for a snip: rectangle, window, full screen or free-form along the top. the snip is copied, and its notification opens it in snipping tool to mark up and save.

## other ways
- win+prtsc saves the whole screen to pictures\screenshots at once · alt+prtsc copies just the window in front
- prtsc opens the same snip bar when "use the print screen key to open screen capture" is on (settings > accessibility > keyboard)

## in the app
- new snip, the mode (rectangle, window, full screen, free-form) and a delay timer (3, 5 or 10 seconds) along the top
- the video camera switch records the screen instead
- pen, highlighter, eraser and crop to mark it up · ctrl+s save · ctrl+c copy
- text actions (newer versions) copies the text out of a snip
