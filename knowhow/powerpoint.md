- alt+q jumps to the ribbon's search box: type any command's name.

## presenting
- f5 from the start · shift+f5 from this slide · alt+f5 with presenter view (your notes and the next slide on your screen)
- during the show: esc ends it · b black screen, w white · type a number and enter to jump to that slide
- ctrl+l laser pointer · ctrl+p pen · e erases the ink · ctrl+a back to the arrow
- rehearse timings and subtitles: slide show tab

## building slides
- ctrl+m new slide · ctrl+d duplicate · ctrl+g group, ctrl+shift+g ungroup · shift while dragging keeps proportions · ctrl+drag makes a copy
- designer (layout ideas): design > designer · slide size (16:9 or 4:3): design > slide size · slide master: view > slide master
- notes: the notes button under the slide, or view > notes · hide a slide: right-click its thumbnail > hide slide
- transitions and animations have their own tabs; animations > animation pane orders them
- reuse slides from another deck: home > new slide > reuse slides
- record with narration: the record tab · export a video or pdf: file > export
