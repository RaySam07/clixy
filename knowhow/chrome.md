- clixy can't open chrome:// pages itself: they press ctrl+l, then type the address (e.g. chrome://settings/clearBrowserData lands right on clearing data). the ⋮ menu at the top right has the rest.

## keys
- ctrl+t new tab · ctrl+shift+t reopens the last closed tab (again for older ones) · ctrl+w close the tab · ctrl+shift+n incognito window
- ctrl+l the address bar · ctrl+h history · ctrl+j downloads · ctrl+d bookmark this page · ctrl+shift+b the bookmarks bar · ctrl+shift+o bookmark manager
- ctrl+tab next tab · ctrl+1 to ctrl+8 that tab, ctrl+9 the last one · ctrl+shift+a search your open tabs
- ctrl+f find on the page · ctrl+p print or save as pdf · ctrl+plus, ctrl+minus, ctrl+0 zoom · f11 full screen
- ctrl+shift+delete clear browsing data · shift+esc chrome's own task manager (which tab is slowing it down) · f12 developer tools

## pages to type in the address bar
- chrome://settings · chrome://settings/clearBrowserData · chrome://password-manager · chrome://extensions · chrome://downloads · chrome://history
- chrome://settings/searchEngines (default search engine) · chrome://settings/onStartup · chrome://settings/content (camera, microphone, notifications, pop-ups per site) · chrome://settings/appearance (home button, font size, page zoom) · chrome://settings/performance (memory saver, energy saver) · chrome://settings/help (version and updates)

## tricks
- dark mode: chrome follows windows' light or dark mode by default; or on the new tab page, customize chrome (bottom right) > light, dark or device
- this site's permissions and cookies: the icon just left of the address
- translate a page: right-click it > translate to english · cast a tab: ⋮ > cast, save and share > cast
- pin or group a tab: right-click the tab · switch profile: the picture at the top right
