- ctrl+. (full stop) lists every keyboard shortcut. settings: the ··· next to your picture at the top right > settings; devices there picks the microphone, speaker and camera.

## in a meeting or call
- ctrl+shift+m mute or unmute · hold ctrl+space to talk while muted · ctrl+shift+o camera on or off
- ctrl+shift+k raise or lower your hand · ctrl+shift+e share your screen
- background blur or a picture: the effects on the join screen before joining; during the meeting, the camera's menu or ··· more > video effects

## around the app
- ctrl+n new chat · ctrl+1, ctrl+2 and so on jump to the apps down the left in order (activity, chat, teams or calendar)
- search: the box at the top
